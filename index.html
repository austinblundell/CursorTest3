    How to play

    • Move with WASD, arrow keys, or the on-screen buttons.
    • Explore forests and ruins to earn experience and gold.
    • Visit towns to rest and buy potions or ethers.
    • Defeat shrine guardians to claim crystals, then challenge the Shadow Spire.
